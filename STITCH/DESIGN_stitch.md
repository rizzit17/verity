## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an uncompromising, terminal-grade intelligence console engineered for mission-critical financial and corporate document verification. The aesthetic rejects decorative softness in favor of brutalist precision, stark zero-radius obsidian panels, high-density telemetry, and hyper-structured typography.

Targeted at equity analysts, forensic auditors, compliance officers, and institutional risk executives, the interface evokes mathematical certainty, absolute transparency, and operational rigor. Every pixel serves auditability. Visual hierarchy is established not through floating organic cards, but via razor-sharp division lines, technical monospaced metadata streams, high-contrast semantic verification beacons, and dense telemetry modules.

The design philosophy combines:
- **Terminal Brutalism:** Exact 0px corners across every view, viewport border, input, and container.
- **Obsidian Architectural Framing:** Deep obsidian and slate depths layered with razor-thin hairline boundaries (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`).
- **Signal-to-Noise Purity:** Space Grotesk anchors high-level executive assertions and analytical nodes, while JetBrains Mono renders all numerical figures, confidence deltas, document hash digests, and cross-reference matrices.
- **Zero Idle Space:** Dense grids filled with live audit trails, extraction timestamps, and contextual token telemetry.

## Colors

The palette operates under a pure dark-mode architecture. Base backgrounds utilize the deepest obsidian `#090D16`, framing elevated analytical panes in slate `#0F172A` and active inspector overlays in `#1E293B`. Hairline borders rendered at `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)` delineate panels with mechanical precision.

Semantic state tokens are high-chroma beacons against the dark substrate:
- **Primary / Reconciliation:** `#8B5CF6` (Violet) coupled with `#A855F7` (Amethyst) designates AI synthetic conclusions, vector merges, and resolved discrepancies.
- **Telemetry Cyan & Electric Indigo:** `#06B6D4` and `#6366F1` are deployed for data pipelines, systemic status markers, and operational controls.
- **Verified / Corroboration:** `#10B981` (Emerald) and `#34D399` indicate mathematically validated fact nodes with high source overlap.
- **Discrepancy / Contradiction:** `#EF4444` (Crimson) and `#F87171` mark numerical variance, clause conflicts, and temporal narrative drift.
- **Review / Latent Flag:** `#F59E0B` (Ochre) and `#FB923C` indicate low-confidence extractions, missing disclosures, or ambiguous provenance.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes distinct technical roles. **Space Grotesk** is reserved exclusively for primary interface headers, systemic mode indicators, and macro document findings. Its geometric, slightly compressed character delivers authority and forward-looking momentum.

**JetBrains Mono** governs the functional core of the system: all tabular values, narrative extraction paragraphs, page-level citations, SEC filing timestamps, SHA checksums, confidence percentages, and filter tags. 

Rules of application:
- Numeric figures must always be formatted in `JetBrains Mono` to ensure tabular vertical alignment across comparisons.
- Micro-labels, metadata markers, and column identifiers must use `label-caps` in full uppercase with expanded letter-spacing (`0.08em`).
- Long-form extracted source text uses `body-md` with relaxed line-height (`1.5`) for rapid audit parsing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is a strict, dense multi-pane workstation layout anchored by a 1px gap/border matrix. Rather than relying on floating islands with wide blank margins, sections abut one another separated by hairline borders (`border-white/10`) to eliminate wasted space.

Key layout principles:
- **Structural Blueprint:** Desktop layouts divide into a left 64px macro-action spine, a 320px persistent document index, an expansive central verification workbench (spanning 60-70% width), and an inspector panel (360px) for source extraction coordinates and citation metadata.
- **Dense Grid Rhythm:** Padding within modules operates on compact 8px increments (`space-2`, `space-3`, `space-4`). Internal tabular rows utilize a fixed 32px or 40px height to maximize data density per screen view.
- **Responsive Adaptation:**
  - **Desktop (>= 1440px):** Unrestricted 4-pane simultaneous visual audit layout.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1439px):** Document index collapses to an accordion drawer; central workbench and citation inspector share a split 50/50 or stacked viewport.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Linear stacked panes with sticky view toggles (`Extracted Text`, `Reconciliation View`, `Citations`). Padding shifts to `panel-padding-tight` (12px).

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects heavy drop shadows and fuzzy ambient blurs. Depth is communicated strictly through surface brightness, razor-sharp hairline borders, and targeted luminescent glow states.

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Deepest background (`#090D16`). Forms the foundational grid trenches and non-interactive gutters.
- **Level 1 (Data Tile / Table Surface):** Solid slate `#0F172A` with an enclosing `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)` perimeter.
- **Level 2 (Active Focus / Selected Module):** `#1E293B` bounded by an active border (`rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.50)` or semantic emerald/crimson).
- **Level 3 (Command Palette / Inspector Flyout):** Surface `#0F172A` with a backdrop blur of `12px` through translucent obsidian (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.85)`), framed by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.20)`.
- **Luminescence / Glow Signaling:** Rather than elevation drop shadows, critical discrepancy states apply a localized razor glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.25)` for errors, and `0 0 12px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)` for verified facts.

## Shapes

The roundedness level is strictly **0**. Every element—from high-level modal containers to buttons, tabs, input fields, confidence tags, and source highlights—features crisp 90-degree corners (`border-radius: 0px`).

This uncompromising geometric profile reinforces the terminal-like nature of the application. It creates an uninterrupted rectilinear flow where adjacent borders fuse cleanly into unified 1px structural frameworks. Precision indicators, such as source-snippet bookmarks or status ribbons, utilize pure rectangular notches, vertical tick marks, or 1px hairline brackets.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Triggers
- **Primary Action:** Solid violet fill (`#8B5CF6`), high-contrast dark text (`#020617`), weight `600`, zero border radius, `0.5rem 1rem` padding. On hover, background shifts to `#A855F7` with subtle neon box-shadow `0 0 8px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.4)`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background, `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`, text `#F8FAFC`. On hover, surface becomes `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` and border transitions to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`.
- **Terminal Tool Trigger:** Small footprint (`28px` height), monospaced text in `JetBrains Mono` (`label-caps`), with square brackets surrounding the label (e.g., `[RUN AUDIT]`).

### 2. Status Chips & Verification Badges
- Zero radius, compact padding (`2px 6px`), monospaced uppercase text (`0.6875rem`).
- **Verified:** Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, border `1px solid #10B981`, text `#34D399`. Prefixed with a solid square glyph `■`.
- **Discrepancy:** Background `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.12)`, border `1px solid #EF4444`, text `#F87171`. Prefixed with `▲`.
- **Reconciled:** Background `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.12)`, border `1px solid #8B5CF6`, text `#A855F7`. Prefixed with `◆`.
- **Warning:** Background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`, border `1px solid #F59E0B`, text `#FB923C`. Prefixed with `!`.

### 3. Verification Lists & Audit Streams
- Monospaced table-row items separated by 1px bottom lines (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).
- Hover state lights up the entire row with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)` and highlights a left vertical 2px indicator in `#8B5CF6` or corresponding semantic color.
- Columns display: `TIMESTAMP (UTC)`, `SOURCE FILE / PAGE`, `RAW ASSERTION`, `DELTA`, and `CONFIDENCE SCORE`.

### 4. Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background `#0F172A`, zero radius, `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`.
- Typography in `JetBrains Mono` (`0.875rem`), placeholder `#64748B`.
- Focus state switches border to `1px solid #8B5CF6` with zero inner shadow; displays a trailing blinking terminal caret block (`█`).

### 5. Document Citation & Fact Cards
- Enclosed in `#0F172A` with a header zone split by a hairline border.
- The card header features document provenance metadata (`10-K FY24 | Page 42 | Paragraph 3`) in `label-caps`.
- Body exhibits extracted assertion text in `body-md`, with discrepancies underlined by a 2px wavy or solid crimson underline.
- Footer displays a horizontal confidence meter: a 10-segment stepped rectangular bar (each step 4px wide, 8px high, 1px gap) glowing emerald up to the validated index.

### 6. Specialized Components: Split Reconciliation Matrix
- A dual-column layout juxtaposing source document extracts directly against synthesized AI outputs.
- Synchronized scroll lines drawn in cyan `#06B6D4` connecting numerical data points from the left raw source document to the right verified model figure.
